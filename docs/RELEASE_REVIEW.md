## Rights and publication

- The compiler states that the definitions were independently researched and written by the compiler, while some third-party quotation may be present. An older KSA-distributed glossary was supplied for terminology, not as the source of the definitions; the original publication details are not currently available. A source citation alone does not grant a right to relicense third-party expression. The underlying source texts and permissions have not been comprehensively checked. Do not represent the rights review as complete.
- Before release, compare definitions with their original sources where available, resolve any substantial verbatim or translated excerpts and third-party rights, and record unavailable sources and necessary permissions. This is not an assertion that any particular entry infringes copyright.
- Compare permanent identifiers to the previous release, inspect changes and run `python3 tools/validate.py`. Its warnings identify entries requiring review, not legal or factual clearance.
- Update version, release date, citation and changelog consistently when the release is approved. Keep the concept DOI and obtain the new version DOI from Zenodo after release; do not alter the existing v1.0.1 archive.
