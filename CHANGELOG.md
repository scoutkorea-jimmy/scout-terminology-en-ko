# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-07
- First compilation: 191 terms, numbered SKT-0001–SKT-0191 in English alphabetical order of `term_en`.
- Sources: BP Media Scout Glossary (bpmedia.net/glossary), five Korea Scout Association regulations (지도자 규정, 포상규정, 진보과정 규정, 제복장구 규정, Youth Hero상 운영규정), a Scout glossary previously distributed by the Korea Scout Association, the KSA Safeguarding Policy (2025), WOSM and World Scout Bureau publications, and Korean press usage.
- Scope: Scout Method, sections and KSA ranks, awards, roles and qualifications, WOSM governance bodies and positions, World Scout Bureau support centres, WOSM regions, events, international organisations, KSA councils, documents, uniform items and pioneering skills. Names of national Scout organisations and faith-based international Scout bodies are excluded.
- Verification status: verified 101, editorial_choice 49, generic_definition 34, corrected 5, partly_verified 2.
- Notable decisions: Personal Progression = 진보과정; World Scout Bureau = 세계사무국 and Global Support Centre = 세계사무국 본부; Regional Support Centre = 지역사무처, Regional Director = 지역 사무처장; Youth Advisor to the World Scout Committee = 세계청년이사 (system discontinued in 2023, last term ended at the 2024 World Scout Conference); Eurasia Scout Region phased out on 1 October 2023; Safe from Harm = 위해로부터안전 (KSA policy name 안전보호정책 recorded separately); terms used in English in Korea keep the English name in `term_ko`.
- Added `term_en_variant` for alternative and former English names (e.g. Africa Regional Office → Africa Support Centre).
- Added Non-formal Education (비정형학습); Team System records its former name Patrol System.
- Abbreviations moved out of `term_en` into `term_en_variant` (e.g. World Scout Foundation / WSF); further abbreviations added (WSB, WSC, SfH and others).
- Added Korea Scout Parliamentary Union (국회스카우트의원연맹).
- Added Korea Scout Foundation (한국스카우트지원재단), National Training Center (중앙훈련원), Badge (패치, with a note that English uses "badge") and Pin (핀뱃지).
- Added Boy Scouts of Korea (한국보이스카우트연맹, BSK), the former name of the Korea Scout Association until 29 December 2001 (KSA official history); founding years added to the Korea Scout Parliamentary Union and Korea Scout Foundation.
- Dates in the Boy Scouts of Korea, Korea Scout Parliamentary Union, Korea Scout Foundation and National Training Center entries checked against the KSA official history. Added National Board (중앙이사회), National Board Member (중앙이사), Council Board Member (지방이사), Council Commissioner (지방커미셔너) and Council Chief Commissioner (지방치프커미셔너).
- Baden-Powell Fellowship: variants BP Fellowship / BP 펠로우십. Chungnam Council rendered 충남세종연맹 (current name on the KSA website).
- Added Asia-Pacific Regional Scout Foundation (아시아-태평양 스카우트 지원재단).
- Added Member Organization (회원기구) and clarified its difference from NSO (회원국) and NSA (회원국협회); added National Headquarters, Buddhist Council, Act on the Promotion of Scouting Activities, World Triennial Plan, Constitution of WOSM, Conference Resolution, Resolutions Committee, Champions for Nature, Tide Turners Plastic Challenge, SCENES and the Asia-Pacific Region Award for Distinguished Service.
- Leading "The" removed from headwords (Scout Method, Scout Promise and Law, World Scout Moot, World Scout Education Congress); the full forms are kept in `term_en_variant`.
- Added from a review of Korean press usage and the 26th World Scout Jamboree (Poland 2027): Scout Movement, Opening/Closing Ceremony, Arrival (입영), Departure (퇴영), Organizing Committee, On-site/Off-site Programme, Day Visitor Programme, Campsite (영지), Subcamp (분단), Pioneering Project (개척물), Camp Chief (야영장); 세계잼버리 added as a variant of World Scout Jamboree.
- Source pass: 154 definitions cited, 30 general definitions marked `not_required`, 7 marked `compiler`; WOSM governance definitions checked against the WSC Standing Orders (2024) and treehouse.scout.org; SCENES updated to its current name (…Nature, Environment and Sustainability). README states the criteria behind `editorial_choice`.

## Unreleased
- Five definitions rewritten in the plain written style used elsewhere (Cub Scouts, Scouts, Venture Scouts, Rover Scouts, World Scout Jamboree); inline "출처:" text moved to the `source` field.
- Korea Scout Parliamentary Union: removed an unverified statement about its relation to the World Scout Parliamentary Union.
